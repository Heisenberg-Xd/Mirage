Who is the Prime Minister of India, and what are some recent policies they enacted?

As of my current knowledge cutoff, the Prime Minister of India is Narendra Modi. He has held this office since May 2014, representing the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP).

Recent notable policies and initiatives under his administration include:

  • Digital India: An initiative to ensure government services are made available to citizens electronically by improved online infrastructure.
  • Make in India: A campaign to encourage companies to manufacture their products in India and incentivize dedicated investments into manufacturing.
  • GST (Goods and Services Tax): A comprehensive, multi-stage, destination-based tax that replaced many indirect taxes in India.
